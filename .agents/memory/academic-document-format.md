---
name: Academic document format
description: Required dissertation and master's-thesis chapter counts and front matter.
---

Dissertations must have a title, a table of contents, and exactly five clearly divided chapters. Master's theses must have a title, a table of contents, and exactly four clearly divided chapters. Neither should be delivered as one undivided stream of prose.

**Why:** Dr. Kuczynski explicitly required these formats for the two academic writing functions.

**How to apply:** Keep chapter counts and document structure fixed when changing academic prompts, generation flow, rendering, or export. Supporting references are back matter, not an additional chapter.
