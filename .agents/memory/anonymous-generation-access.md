name: Public previews and paid answers
description: Production users need Google login and payment for complete answers, except the designated owner.
---
Public production users may try the app without signing in, but receive only partial previews. Complete answers require both Google login and confirmed payment, except for the explicitly designated owner.

**Why:** Dr. Kuczynski explicitly replaced the previous unrestricted anonymous-generation policy with paid complete answers. He requested half of shorter answers and less than one tenth of very long answers for unpaid users.

**How to apply:** Keep the product visible and available to try, label partial previews clearly, and enforce output limits on the server. Follow the separate development/production owner exception; do not make the owner pay.
