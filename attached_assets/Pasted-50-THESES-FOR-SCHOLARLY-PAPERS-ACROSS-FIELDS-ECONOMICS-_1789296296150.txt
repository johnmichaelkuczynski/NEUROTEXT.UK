50 THESES FOR SCHOLARLY PAPERS — ACROSS FIELDS

ECONOMICS AND FINANCE
1. Volatility-decay in leveraged ETFs is not a cost of leverage but a cost of rebalancing frequency, and the two are separable by contract design.
2. Index inclusion effects have inverted since 2010: passive flows now create predictable post-inclusion underperformance rather than the premium documented in the 1990s literature.
3. Central bank forward guidance loses efficacy not through loss of credibility but through saturation of the information it conveys.
4. Minimum wage employment effects are mediated almost entirely by local labor-market concentration, which reconciles the conflicting empirical literature.
5. The equity risk premium is an artifact of survivorship in the sample of countries whose markets remained continuously tradable.
6. Options-market implied distributions systematically misprice tail risk in sectors with high capital-expenditure cyclicality.

COMPUTER SCIENCE AND AI
7. Large language model "reasoning" gains from chain-of-thought are gains in search, not in inference, and are therefore bounded by the base model's retrieval coverage.
8. Benchmark saturation in machine learning reflects benchmark construction bias rather than model capability, and can be detected by item-response-theory analysis of test items.
9. Retrieval-augmented generation degrades factual accuracy when retrieval confidence is uncorrelated with generation confidence — the standard case.
10. Model interpretability methods that rely on gradient attribution are provably uninformative for architectures with attention-based routing.
11. Software complexity metrics predict defect rates only through their correlation with author turnover, and drop to zero predictive value when turnover is controlled.
12. Formal verification adoption is limited by specification cost, not proof cost, and the field has systematically misallocated research effort as a result.

LINGUISTICS
13. Grammaticality judgments are graded measurements of processing cost, not binary measurements of competence, and generative syntax's data source is therefore misdescribed.
14. Language change accelerates at the boundaries of dense social networks rather than at their centers, contrary to the standard diffusion model.
15. Second-language attrition follows a different curve from first-language attrition, indicating distinct storage rather than distinct access.
16. Writing systems constrain phonological awareness in ways that contaminate cross-linguistic phonology research conducted on literate populations.

PSYCHOLOGY AND COGNITIVE SCIENCE
17. The replication crisis in social psychology is a measurement crisis: the constructs, not the effects, fail to replicate.
18. Expertise is domain-general in its acquisition mechanism and domain-specific only in its content, which predicts transfer under conditions current models rule out.
19. Working memory capacity limits are limits on interference resolution, not on storage, and the distinction is testable via similarity-graded load.
20. Intelligence test scores predict life outcomes largely through their correlation with credentialing thresholds rather than through cognitive performance itself.
21. Cognitive biases documented in laboratory tasks are rational responses to the ecological statistics of the laboratory, not to those of natural environments.

EDUCATION
22. Lecture-based instruction outperforms active learning for material with high prerequisite density, reversing the standard finding.
23. Assessment validity in higher education has collapsed not because of AI but because assessment instruments were already measuring compliance rather than competence.
24. Spaced repetition's advantage disappears for material that is used rather than recalled, which limits its applicability far more than the literature admits.
25. Credential inflation is driven by employer screening costs rather than by student demand, and is therefore unresponsive to tuition policy.

HISTORY AND POLITICAL SCIENCE
26. The historiography of industrialization overweights technological invention relative to changes in contract enforcement.
27. Democratic backsliding follows a legislative rather than an executive pathway in states with strong judicial review, which inverts the standard early-warning indicators.
28. Coalition instability in parliamentary systems is better predicted by intra-party factional count than by the effective number of parties.
29. Archival silence is not merely an absence of evidence but a positive artifact of record-keeping incentives, and can be modeled as such.
30. Wartime mobilization reshapes state fiscal capacity permanently only where it coincides with prior administrative penetration of the countryside.

LAW
31. Textualism and purposivism converge in outcome and diverge only in the allocation of justificatory burden, which explains their persistence as rival doctrines.
32. Qualified immunity doctrine functions as an information-forcing rule rather than an immunity, and its critics and defenders both misdescribe it.
33. Patent claim breadth and litigation frequency are related non-monotonically, with the peak at intermediate breadth — contrary to both the pro- and anti-patent literature.
34. Standards of proof are not thresholds on belief but allocations of error cost, and jury instructions systematically communicate the former.

BIOLOGY AND MEDICINE
35. Antimicrobial resistance evolution in clinical settings is driven more by dosing interval than by total antibiotic volume.
36. The microbiome literature's causal claims rest on compositional data analyzed with methods invalid for compositional data.
37. Aging research conflates two distinct phenomena — damage accumulation and program-driven decline — that have opposite therapeutic implications.
38. Clinical trial endpoints selected for regulatory tractability have systematically diverged from endpoints patients value, and the divergence is measurable.
39. Convergent evolution is overreported because morphological similarity is scored by researchers already committed to a phylogenetic hypothesis.

PHYSICS AND STATISTICS
40. Statistical significance thresholds function as publication filters rather than inference rules, and the distinction explains the shape of published effect-size distributions.
41. Causal inference from observational data is more reliable than from underpowered randomized trials across a definable and estimable range of conditions.
42. Model selection criteria (AIC, BIC) encode incompatible assumptions about the true model's presence in the candidate set, and applied work rarely respects either.
43. Emergence in physics is an epistemic relation between descriptions at different scales, not an ontological relation between levels of reality.

MUSIC AND THE ARTS
44. Tonal function is a statistical expectation structure, and historical changes in harmonic practice track changes in listener exposure statistics rather than in theory.
45. Counterpoint rules are compression heuristics: they minimize the information required to track independent voices, which explains their cross-cultural analogues.
46. Recorded-music distribution economics have made album-length coherence a cost rather than a value, with measurable effects on compositional structure since 2015.
47. Literary canon formation is driven by pedagogical utility — teachability — rather than by evaluative consensus, and the two can be empirically separated.

SOCIOLOGY AND ANTHROPOLOGY
48. Urban density affects social trust through exposure variance rather than exposure frequency, reconciling the contradictory findings in the contact-hypothesis literature.
49. Kinship terminology systems encode inheritance rules rather than biological relatedness, and mismatches between the two are systematic, not residual.
50. Online platform moderation produces norm displacement rather than norm enforcement, and displaced norms return with higher intensity in lower-visibility venues.
